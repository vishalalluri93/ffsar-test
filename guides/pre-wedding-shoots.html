Guide · Moinabad, near Hyderabad

A pre-wedding shoot in the gardens at Firefox.

Firefox Sports & Resorts welcomes pre-wedding shoots in its gardens, whether or not your wedding is here. The estate is 15 acres at Moinabad, about 20 km from Hyderabad, and the day is booked with one call to the desk.

Where you can shoot

Every garden space on the estate is open to your shoot. A few that photographers tend to reach for:

  • The swings garden: wooden swings hung from a long timber pergola, set on open lawn.
  • The palm walks: long paved paths between palms and shade trees, which draw the eye straight down the frame.
  • The tiled pergola: swings under a timber pergola over a checked tile floor, framed by flowering beds.
  • The open lawns: wide lawns under old shade trees, with room for full-length portraits.
  • The neem walk: a narrow path under a canopy of neem.

Because it is one estate, you can move between all of them in a single session, with no driving between locations. See the estate.

Who can book

Any couple. You do not need to hold your wedding at Firefox to shoot here. If you are planning it here too, the shoot is a good way to get to know the gardens before the day. Weddings at Firefox.

Planning the day

  • Getting here: about 20 km from Hyderabad, with parking for 500. Directions on Google Maps.
  • The light: outdoor shoots usually plan around the softer light of early morning and late afternoon. Tell the desk the time you have in mind.
  • Your team: tell the desk how many people are coming (the two of you, the photographer and any crew) and how many outfits you plan, so the day can be planned around you.
  • Staying over: there are 110 rooms on the estate if you would rather arrive the evening before. The hotel.

How to book

Call the desk or send an enquiry with your date, the time you would like to start and the size of your team. The desk confirms the date and the venue charge for your shoot.
